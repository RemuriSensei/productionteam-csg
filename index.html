PRODUCTION TEAM RULES AND REGULATIONS

Please review all official rules before submitting your application. Adherence to guidelines is strictly enforced for all active team members.

1 Attendance and Punctuality

  • All members must arrive 15–30 minutes before the scheduled call time.
  • Members who arrive late must inform the team leader and provide a valid reason.
  • Repeated tardiness will result in a warning and may lead to removal from the Production Team.
  • Members who are absent during an event without a valid reason or prior notice may be removed from the Production Team.
  • Members who need to be absent must inform the team leader as early as possible.

2 Commitment to Assigned Duties

  • Members must attend events and activities assigned to them.
  • Members must complete their assigned tasks responsibly and on time.
  • Leaving the assigned area or event without permission is prohibited.
  • Failure to perform assigned responsibilities without a valid reason may result in removal from the team.

3 Proper Behavior

  • All members must treat teammates, guests, organizers, and other participants with respect and professionalism.
  • Bullying, harassment, discrimination, fighting, or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated.
  • Serious misconduct may result in immediate removal from the Production Team.

4 Teamwork and Communication

  • Members must cooperate and communicate properly with the team.
  • Important announcements and instructions must be acknowledged and followed.
  • Members should not create unnecessary conflicts or disrupt the work of others.
  • Refusal to cooperate repeatedly may result in removal from the team.

5 Removal from the Production Team

A member may be removed from the Production Team for serious or repeated violations, including:

  • Repeated lateness.
  • Unexcused absence during events.
  • Leaving an event without permission.
  • Failure to perform assigned duties.
  • Disrespectful or inappropriate behavior.
  • Bullying or harassment.
  • Repeated refusal to follow reasonable team instructions.
  • Any action that seriously affects the safety, performance, or reputation of the Production Team.

Final Rule & Code of Conduct

"All members are expected to demonstrate punctuality, responsibility, respect, teamwork, and commitment. The Production Team has the right to remove members who repeatedly violate these rules or commit serious offenses, following the organization's established process."
